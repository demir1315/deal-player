# Deal Player

Deal Player is a commercial IPTV media player for iOS and macOS.

Users add their own legally authorized M3U or Xtream playlists. Deal Player does not provide, host, sell, or redistribute video content.

TMDB metadata is used to enrich movie and TV detail pages with:

- Posters and backdrops
- Titles and descriptions
- Ratings
- Cast information
- Movie and TV logos

The application is currently in development and is planned for distribution through the Apple App Store.
